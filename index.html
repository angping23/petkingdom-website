💡 Pet Care Tips

Expert advice to keep your beloved pets healthy, happy, and thriving.

🍖

Proper Nutrition & Diet

Feed your pets age-appropriate, high-quality food. Dogs need protein-rich meals twice daily; cats require taurine-rich food. Avoid feeding pets human food like onions, grapes, or chocolate.

🏃

Daily Exercise & Play

Dogs need at least 30 minutes of walking daily. Cats benefit from interactive toys. Regular exercise prevents obesity and keeps your pet mentally stimulated and happy.

🛁

Grooming & Hygiene

Bathe dogs every 2–4 weeks using pet-safe shampoo. Brush their teeth 2–3 times a week. Trim nails monthly. Regular grooming prevents infections and keeps your pet comfortable.

💉

Vaccinations & Vet Visits

Keep up with your pet's vaccination schedule. Annual vet check-ups can catch health issues early. Deworm dogs and cats every 3 months for parasite prevention.

💧

Fresh Water Always

Ensure your pet always has access to clean, fresh water. Change it at least once daily. Dehydration can cause serious health problems, especially in hot Malaysian weather.

😴

Comfortable Rest Space

Provide a clean, comfortable resting space away from loud noises and direct sunlight. Dogs and cats need 12–14 hours of sleep per day for optimal health.
